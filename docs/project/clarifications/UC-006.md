# UC-006 Explore products of an edition

Covers BRU-018 (seed visibility) for listings.

## Endpoints and access
- `GET /seeds`, `GET /flowers`, `GET /fruits`, each with `?editionId=`. The SRS doesn't mention the `hackathonId`
  filter, so it is out of scope.
- No `editionId` → `400`. Unknown edition → `404`.
- `PLANNED` edition: guests and participants → `404`, like its details (UC-003).
- A seed that belongs to several editions appears in the list of each of them.

Decisions: D-042.

## Which products are listed
| Role | Products |
|---|---|
| Guests and participants (authors included) | `PUBLISHED` only. Authors get their own drafts through UC-019. |
| Staff | every state |

- Flowers and fruits of a private hackathon are listed as normal once published. Only the hackathon itself is private.
- This differs from edition details (UC-003), where unpublished fruits are shown to nobody.

Decisions: D-042.

## Item shape (exact, every role)
| Type | Fields |
|---|---|
| Seed | `id`, `state`, `title`, `mainImage`, `branchesOfKnowledge`, `authors` |
| Flower | `id`, `state`, `title`, `mainImage`, `authors` |
| Fruit | `id`, `state`, `title`, `mainImage`, `authors` |

- `mainImage` is a link.
- Each author is exactly `id`, `name`, `surname`, never an email.
- Flower authors = members of its co-creation team.

Decisions: D-042.
