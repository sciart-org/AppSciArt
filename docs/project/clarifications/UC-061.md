# UC-061 Create co-creation teams

## Teams and seeds
- One seed per team. Teams of the same hackathon can't share a seed.
- Not every seed needs a team.
- May change later.

## Starting team work
- Same membership and speaker rules as groups (`UC-058.md`): `TEAM_WORK` can't start while a team has no speaker
  (BRU-032), or while an assisting participant has no team (pending SRS change, D-049). Naming a second speaker
  replaces the first.
