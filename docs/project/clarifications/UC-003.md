# UC-003 Edition details

Formerly testing plan §9 Q13.

## Access
- Details of an edition that isn't announced yet (`PLANNED`, e.g. `GET /editions/{id}`):
  - guests and participants → `404`, so its existence stays hidden;
  - staff → `200` with its details (descriptions and logo may still be missing).
- Unknown id → `404`.

Decisions: D-024.

## Fields (exact)
- Guests and participants: `id`, `state`, `name`, `year`, `logo`, `shortDescription`, `longDescription`, `fruits`,
  plus `catalogLink`.
  - `catalogLink` is compulsory for `PUBLISHED` editions; absent or `null` otherwise, either is fine.
  - For `PLANNED` editions (staff only), the descriptions and logo may be missing.
- Staff also get `driveLink`.
- "Finished" = `PUBLISHED` only. A `CLOSED` edition has no catalogue yet.

Decisions: D-024.

## Fruits
- The details of a `PUBLISHED` edition include `fruits`, each exactly `id`, `mainImage`, `title`, the same for
  staff.
- Only published fruits are listed, for every role (staff included).
- Non-published editions carry no fruits.

Decisions: D-025, D-026.
