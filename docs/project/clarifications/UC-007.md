# UC-007 Product details

Covers BRU-018 (seed visibility) for details.

## Access
- `GET /seeds/{id}`, `GET /flowers/{id}`, `GET /fruits/{id}`. Unknown id → `404`.
- Published product: every role, guests included.
- Unpublished product:
  - staff → `200`;
  - evaluators → `200` for flowers and fruits of the hackathons they evaluate (not seeds);
  - its authors → `200`;
  - other participants and guests → `404`, so its existence stays hidden.
- A flower or fruit of a private hackathon is visible once published.
- An unpublished product's content (everything except `id`, `state`, `title`) may still be missing.

Decisions: D-043, D-045, D-046.

## Fields (exact)
Staff and the product's authors also get `driveLink` and `template`. So do evaluators, for the flowers and fruits of
the hackathons they evaluate. Nobody else gets them: an inspiring scientist only for their own seeds (as an author).
The same holds for each related product. (`template` may be removed later; keep it for now.)

| Type | Required | Optional |
|---|---|---|
| Seed | `id`, `title`, `state`, `branchesOfKnowledge`, `seedPDF` (a link), `mainImage`, `authors` | `videoLink`, `presentationLink`, `podcastLink`, `otherImages` |
| Flower | `id`, `title`, `concept`, `state`, `seed`, `mainImage`, `authors` | `prototypeImages`, `inspirationImages`, `presentation`, `video` |
| Fruit | `id`, `title`, `state`, `authorVision`, `curatorVision`, `flower`, `seed`, `mainImage`, `authors` | `otherImages` |

- Related products are their own details, as the same viewer gets them, with the same field rules:
  - flower `seed`: what `GET /seeds/{id}` returns. `driveLink` and `template` only for staff and the seed's authors.
  - fruit `flower`: what `GET /flowers/{id}` returns, **without** its `seed` (the fruit has its own `seed`).
  - fruit `seed`: what `GET /seeds/{id}` returns, plus `description`. `description` is the seed description stored
    for that fruit (IRQ-004), not the seed's own data.
- Each author is exactly `id`, `name`, `surname`, never an email.
- Authors:
  - seed: the inspiring scientists;
  - flower: its co-creation team;
  - fruit: the fruit's authors.
- Images and documents are links.

Decisions: D-043, D-044, D-045, D-046.
