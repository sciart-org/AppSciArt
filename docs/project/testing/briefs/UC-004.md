## Vocabulary
Not announced = `PLANNED`; announced = `OPEN`; closed = `CLOSED` + `PREPARING`; ongoing = `CLOSED` + a phase from
`GROUP_CREATION` to `TEAM_PRESENTATION`; ended = `FINISHED`. Guest = no token; participant = authenticated, no
staff role; staff = facilitator, designer, administrator.

## Test cases
Visibility
- **B1** Guest gets an empty list when no hackathon exists.
- **B2** Guest gets an empty list when only not-announced and private hackathons exist.
- **B3** Guest sees announced, closed, ongoing and ended hackathons.
- **B4** Guest never sees not-announced hackathons.
- **B5** Guest never sees private hackathons, whatever their state.
- **B6** Participant sees exactly the same hackathons as a guest.
- **B7** Each staff role sees not-announced and private hackathons too.

Fields
- **B8** Guest items have exactly the allowed fields, with the required ones present.
- **B9** An online hackathon's item has `location` absent or `null`; an on-site one has its location.
- **B10** `editionName` is the hackathon's edition name.
- **B11** Guest items have no `isEnrolled`; participant items all carry a boolean `isEnrolled`, no staff-only
  field and nothing outside the participant set.
- **B12** Staff items have no field outside the staff set.
- **B13** No item carries `meetLink`, for guest, participant or staff.

Enrolment mark
- **B14** A participant's enrolled hackathons (announced, ongoing, ended) are marked `isEnrolled: true`; others `false`.
- **B15** An assisting participant's hackathon is marked as enrolled.
- **B16** Being an evaluator of a hackathon doesn't mark it as enrolled.
