## Vocabulary
Not announced = `PLANNED`; announced = `ACTIVE`; closed = `CLOSED`; finished / published = `PUBLISHED`. Guest = no
token; participant = authenticated, no staff role; staff = facilitator, designer, administrator.

## Test cases
Access
- **B1** Guest gets the details of an announced edition.
- **B2** Guest gets the details of closed and published editions.
- **B3** Guest and participant asking for a not-announced edition → `404`.
- **B4** Each staff role gets the details of a not-announced edition, even with descriptions and logo missing
  (absent or `null`).
- **B5** Unknown id → `404`, for a guest and for staff.

Fields
- **B6** Guest details have exactly `id`, `state`, `name`, `year`, `logo`, `shortDescription`, `longDescription`,
  `fruits`, plus `catalogLink`; `longDescription` is the edition's full description.
- **B7** Participant details have the same field set; no `driveLink`.
- **B8** Staff details have the same set plus `driveLink`, and nothing else.
- **B9** A published edition's details include `catalogLink`, and it is the edition's catalogue link.
- **B10** Announced and closed editions' details have `catalogLink` absent or `null`.

Fruits
- **B11** The details of announced, closed and published editions list their published fruits, each exactly `id`,
  `mainImage` (a link), `title`; the same fruits and shape for guest, participant and staff.
- **B12** Unpublished fruits of announced, closed and published editions are listed for nobody, staff included.
- **B13** Fruits of other editions are not listed.
- ~~B14~~ Announced and closed editions carry no fruits. Withdrawn 2026-10-23: every non-planned edition lists its
  published fruits (D-041); not-announced editions can't have fruits, so there is nothing to test.

## Fixture notes
- `anEdition` and its shortcuts; `aPublishedEdition` comes with a catalogue link. Pass `null` for a missing field
  and `logo: false` for no logo.
- B4 needs a not-announced edition with no descriptions and no logo (`null` descriptions, `logo: false`).
- Give announced, closed and published editions both published and unpublished fruits (B11, B12).
- `aFruit({ edition, state })` builds the whole chain; fruits of a closed or published edition need its hackathons
  ended (pass a `FINISHED` hackathon or let `aFruit` build it).
