# UC-052 Submit seed ratings → `backend/tests/acceptance/seedRatings.test.js`

> Scope (2026-11-24): submitting and reading ratings: who may, when, and only once. The rest of UC-050 and UC-052
> (the rating scale, rating notes, ratings kept until team creation) is tested at step A5; this file is extended
> then.

## Requirement
- UC-052 Submit seed ratings (`srs-export.txt` ~line 1730): "…when a participant wants to submit their final
  ratings for all presented seeds." Precondition: "All conceptual maps describing some seed have been presented in
  the ongoing hackathon."
- UC-050 Rate seed (~line 1696): a participant rates a seed being presented.
- BRU-023 Seed ratings (~line 3040): "Every participant may rate every presented seed, including the one presented
  by their own group."
- BRU-042 Ratings submission (~line 3051): "Participant ratings can no longer be modified after submission."
- BRU-025 Evaluators' activity (~line 2779): evaluators observe, and act only on rubrics.

## Clarifications
- `docs/project/clarifications/UC-052.md`: "When a participant can submit" (after every group has presented, every
  presented seed rated) and "Who sees ratings" (staff and evaluators see every rating; each participant only their
  own).
- `docs/project/clarifications/UC-050.md` "What can be rated": ratings can change until the participant submits.
- `docs/project/clarifications/UC-047.md` and `UC-057.md`: enrolled participants take part whether or not they are
  assisting.
- `docs/project/clarifications/general.md` "Actors".

## Vocabulary
- Ongoing hackathon in group presentations = `anOngoingHackathonAt('GROUP_PRESENTATION', …)`.
- Joining = `socketAs(user, { hackathon })`. Staff and evaluators also join the management room.
- Every event below is answered by the server once handled, refused or not: send it with
  `await socket.timeout(2000).emitWithAck(event, ...args)`. A refusal sends `error_message` (a text) to the sender
  and changes nothing.
- "Every group has presented" = a facilitator has sent `enable_ratings_submission` (no arguments).
- `submit_ratings` with a list of `{ seedId, rating }`, one per group's seed; `rating` is a number (use `0.8`; the
  scale is out of scope). Staff and evaluators who joined receive `ratings`.
- `get_ratings` (no arguments): the sender receives `ratings`.
- `ratings` is an object keyed by each submitting participant's user id (`participant(n).id`), holding the list they
  submitted. Check only the entries of the participants in the test.

## Test cases
Submitting
- **B1** After ratings open, a participant who submits a rating for every group's seed: the joined facilitator
  receives `ratings` with that participant's list under their id.
- **B2** The joined evaluator receives the same `ratings`.
- **B3** An enrolled participant who isn't assisting can submit (UC-057).
- **B4** A participant can rate their own group's seed (BRU-023).

Reading
- **B5** A facilitator's `get_ratings` returns the submitted list under the participant's id.
- **B6** An evaluator's `get_ratings` returns the same.
- **B7** A participant's `get_ratings` gets `error_message`.

Refused submissions (each: `error_message`, staff receive no `ratings`, and `get_ratings` by a facilitator has no
entry for that participant, or still the first one)
- **B8** Submitting before ratings open.
- **B9** Submitting without a rating for every group's seed.
- **B10** Submitting a second time: the first list is kept (BRU-042).
- **B11** An evaluator submitting (BRU-025).
- **B12** A facilitator submitting (the actor is a participant).

Bad requests
- **B13** `submit_ratings` with a malformed value (not a list, a `seedId` that isn't an id, a `rating` that isn't a
  number) gets `error_message`, and the server keeps running (`serverCrashes()` is empty).
- **B14** `submit_ratings` and `get_ratings` from a socket that joined no hackathon get `error_message`, and the
  server keeps running.

## Fixture notes
- `anOngoingHackathonAt('GROUP_PRESENTATION', { edition, seeds, participants, assisting, evaluators })`, two groups
  (`aGroup`) on two seeds of that edition (`aPublishedSeed`); the seeds' ids are the `seedId`s.
- Use a new hackathon per test: ratings are kept per hackathon.
- A user can be evaluator of only one hackathon in the test database: use a different participant as evaluator for
  each hackathon.
