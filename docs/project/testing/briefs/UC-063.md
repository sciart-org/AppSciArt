## Vocabulary
- Ongoing hackathon in team presentations = `anOngoingHackathonAt('TEAM_PRESENTATION', …)`.
- Joining = `socketAs(user, { hackathon })`. Participants join the hackathon's room; staff and evaluators also join
  its management room.
- Every event below is answered by the server once handled, refused or not: send it with
  `await socket.timeout(2000).emitWithAck(event, ...args)`. A refusal sends `error_message` (a text) to the sender
  and changes nothing. Broadcasts are not sent back to the sender.
- Teams are numbered 1, 2, … within the hackathon (`aTeam({ number })`).
- `set_presenting_team` with a team number: everyone else who joined receives `new_presenting_team` with that
  number.
- `get_team_presenting_state`: the sender receives `team_presenting_state`; its `presentingTeam` is the current
  number. Check only that field.

## Test cases
- **B1** A facilitator sets team 2: participants who joined receive `new_presenting_team` with `2`, whether or not
  they are assisting or in a team (UC-055, D-075).
- **B2** Another staff member and an evaluator who joined receive `new_presenting_team` with `2`.
- **B3** A designer and an administrator can each set the presenting team.
- **B4** A client that joins after the change gets `presentingTeam` `2` from `get_team_presenting_state` (IRQ-006).
- **B5** A participant's `set_presenting_team` gets `error_message`; nobody receives `new_presenting_team`, and the
  presenting team is unchanged when read back.
- **B6** An evaluator's `set_presenting_team` gets `error_message`, with the same checks (BRU-025).
- **B7** A participant who joined another hackathon doesn't receive this hackathon's `new_presenting_team`.
- **B8** `set_presenting_team` with a malformed team number (`0`, `-1`, `'2'`, missing) gets `error_message`, the
  presenting team is unchanged, and the server keeps running (`serverCrashes()` is empty).
- **B9** `set_presenting_team` and `get_team_presenting_state` from a socket that joined no hackathon each get
  `error_message`, and the server keeps running.
- **B10** `set_presenting_team` with a well-formed number that matches no team of the hackathon (`99`) gets
  `error_message`, nobody receives `new_presenting_team`, and the presenting team is unchanged when read back.

## Fixture notes
- `anOngoingHackathonAt('TEAM_PRESENTATION', { edition, seeds, participants, assisting, evaluators })`, two teams
  (`aTeam`, numbers 1 and 2) on two seeds of that edition (`aPublishedSeed`), plus an enrolled participant in no
  team.
- B7: a second ongoing hackathon with its own participant.
- Use a new hackathon per test: the presenting team is kept per hackathon.
- A user can be evaluator of only one hackathon in the test database: use a different participant as evaluator for
  each hackathon.
