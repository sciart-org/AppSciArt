# UC-047 Enter ongoing hackathon → `backend/tests/acceptance/enterHackathon.test.js`

> Scope (2026-11-24): joining an ongoing hackathon's real-time rooms: who may, when, and what each one then
> receives. The rest of UC-047 (the hackathon page and its sections) is tested at step A5; this file is extended
> then.

## Requirement
- UC-047 Enter ongoing hackathon (`srs-export.txt` ~line 1638): "…when a participant wants to enter an ongoing
  hackathon in which they are enrolled." Precondition: "At least one ongoing hackathon in which the actor is
  enrolled must exist." Pending SRS change (D-049 Q4): entering doesn't mark assistance.
- BRU-048 Hackathon entering (~line 2724): "A hackathon can only be entered by administrators, facilitators, and its
  evaluators and enrolled participants."
- BRU-025 Evaluators' activity (~line 2779): "Evaluators can observe management activity but cannot act on any of it
  except rubric-related operations."

## Clarifications
- `docs/project/clarifications/UC-047.md` "Who can enter": any enrolled participant, assisting or not.
- `docs/project/clarifications/UC-057.md`: assistance is only an indicator for staff.
- `docs/project/clarifications/UC-066.md`: evaluators see the management view the same as facilitators, read-only.
- `docs/project/clarifications/general.md` "Actors": staff = facilitators, designers and administrators.
- User, 2026-11-24:
  - Real-time rooms can be joined only while the hackathon is ongoing (`CLOSED`, any phase from `PREPARING` on),
    never when it is not announced, announced or ended.

## Vocabulary
- Ongoing hackathon = state `CLOSED`, any phase (`aClosedHackathon` for `PREPARING`, `anOngoingHackathonAt`).
- Joining = `socketAs(user, { hackathon })`: it emits `join_hackathon_rooms` with the hackathon's id and waits for
  the server's answer. To send it by hand (a second join, a malformed id), use
  `await socket.timeout(2000).emitWithAck('join_hackathon_rooms', value)`.
- A refused join: the socket receives `error_message` (a text) and joins nothing.
- What a joined socket receives, used here to tell which rooms it is in:
  - Participant updates: `PUT /hackathons/{hackathonId}?broadcast=ALL` by a facilitator sends `hackathon:updated`
    to everyone who joined (participants, evaluators and staff).
  - Management updates: the same `PUT` with `broadcast=STAFF` sends `hackathon:updated` only to staff and the
    hackathon's evaluators.
  - Use a field any staff member may change on an ongoing hackathon, e.g. `{ location: 'Granada' }`.
- Only who receives the event matters here, not its content.

## Test cases
Who joins
- **B1** An enrolled participant who isn't assisting joins and receives the participant updates.
- **B2** An enrolled, assisting participant joins and receives the participant updates.
- **B3** An enrolled participant doesn't receive the management updates.
- **B4** An evaluator of the hackathon joins and receives the management updates.
- **B5** A facilitator, a designer and an administrator each join and receive the management updates.

Who is refused
- **B6** A registered user who isn't enrolled gets `error_message` and receives no participant updates.
- **B7** An inspiring scientist of the hackathon's edition who isn't enrolled gets `error_message` and receives no
  participant updates (BRU-048 doesn't list scientists).
- **B8** An evaluator of another hackathon gets `error_message` and receives no updates of this one.

When
- **B9** Joining works in `PREPARING` and in a later phase (e.g. `TEAM_WORK`).
- **B10** A facilitator gets `error_message` joining a not announced, an announced and an ended hackathon.
- **B14** An enrolled participant gets `error_message` joining an announced and an ended hackathon.

Bad requests
- **B11** A `join_hackathon_rooms` with a malformed value (not an id, missing, a number) gets `error_message`, and
  the server keeps running (`serverCrashes()` is empty).
- **B12** After a malformed join, the same socket can still join the hackathon properly and receives its
  participant updates.
- **B13** A well-formed id of a hackathon that doesn't exist gets `error_message`.

## Fixture notes
- `aClosedHackathon({ participants, assisting, evaluators })`, `anOngoingHackathonAt(phase, …)`,
  `aNotAnnouncedHackathon`, `anAnnouncedHackathon`, `anEndedHackathon`.
- Scientist: `aScientistOf(edition, user)` with the hackathon's edition (create the edition first and pass it as
  `edition`).
- Evaluators are participants made evaluators (`evaluators: [participant(n)]`).
- Wait for absent events with `noEventArrives`; wait for present ones with `nextEvent` set up before acting.
