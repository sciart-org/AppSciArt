## Vocabulary
- Product types: seed, flower, fruit (`GET /seeds`, `GET /flowers`, `GET /fruits`, each with `?editionId=`).
- Published = state `PUBLISHED`. Unpublished seed = `IN_PROGRESS` or `IN_REVIEW`; unpublished flower or fruit =
  `IN_BLANK`, `IN_PROGRESS` or `IN_REVIEW`.
- Editions: not announced = `PLANNED`; announced = `ACTIVE`; closed = `CLOSED`; published = `PUBLISHED`.
- Guest = no token; participant = authenticated, no staff role; staff = facilitator, designer, administrator.

## Test cases
Selecting the edition
- **B1** Guest lists the published seeds, flowers and fruits of an announced edition (one test per type).
- **B2** Guest lists the published products of closed and published editions.
- **B3** Guest and participant listing the products of a not-announced edition → `404`, for each type.
- **B4** Listing without `editionId` → `400`, for each type.
- **B5** Listing with an unknown `editionId` → `404`, for each type.
- **B6** Products of other editions are not listed.
- **B7** A seed that belongs to two editions is listed in both.

Which products are listed
- **B8** Guest and participant don't get unpublished seeds (BRU-018).
- **B9** Guest and participant don't get unpublished flowers or fruits.
- **B10** A seed's author (a participant) doesn't get their own unpublished seed in the list.
- **B11** Each staff role gets the products of every state, for each type.
- **B12** Published flowers and fruits of a private hackathon are listed for a guest.
- **B18** An evaluator of a hackathon:
  - gets that hackathon's flowers and fruits of every state, with the same item shape;
  - doesn't get the unpublished flowers and fruits of a hackathon they don't evaluate;
  - doesn't get unpublished seeds.

Item shape (every role)
- **B13** Seed items have exactly `id`, `state`, `title`, `mainImage` (a link), `branchesOfKnowledge`, `authors`.
- **B14** Flower items have exactly `id`, `state`, `title`, `mainImage` (a link), `authors`.
- **B15** Fruit items have exactly `id`, `state`, `title`, `mainImage` (a link), `authors`.
- **B16** Each author is exactly `id`, `name`, `surname`, and the authors are the product's own: a seed's inspiring
  scientists, a flower's team members, a fruit's authors.
- **B17** The item shape is the same for guest, participant and staff.

## Fixture notes
- Seeds: `aSeed({ edition, authors, state })`, `aPublishedSeed`; `aSeedOfEdition(edition, seed)` for B7. Seeds have
  a main image by default.
- Flowers: a hackathon of the edition, in a team phase or ended, with the seed assigned (`seeds: [seed]`) and the
  members enrolled; then `aTeam({ hackathon, seed, members, flowerState })`. The flower's edition is its hackathon's.
- Fruits: `aFruit({ edition, hackathon, authors, state })` builds the whole chain and returns its `flower`, `seed`
  and `hackathon`.
- Closed and published editions only have ended hackathons. A private hackathon: `isPrivate: true` (B12).
- Authors are participants (`participant(n)`).
- Evaluators: `anEvaluatorOf(hackathon, user)`, or `evaluators: [user]` on the hackathon (B18). A flower's hackathon is
  its team's; a fruit's is the `hackathon` `aFruit` returns.
