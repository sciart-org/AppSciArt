## Vocabulary
- `GET /seeds/{id}`, `GET /flowers/{id}`, `GET /fruits/{id}`.
- Published = state `PUBLISHED`. Unpublished seed = `IN_PROGRESS` or `IN_REVIEW`; unpublished flower or fruit =
  `IN_BLANK`, `IN_PROGRESS` or `IN_REVIEW`.
- Authors: a seed's inspiring scientists; a flower's co-creation team members; a fruit's authors.
- Guest = no token; participant = authenticated, no staff role; staff = facilitator, designer, administrator.

## Test cases
Access
- **B1** Guest gets the details of a published seed, flower and fruit (one test per type).
- **B2** Guest and participant asking for an unpublished seed → `404` (BRU-018).
- **B3** Guest and participant asking for an unpublished flower or fruit → `404`.
- **B4** Each staff role gets the details of an unpublished seed, flower and fruit.
- **B5** An author gets the details of their own unpublished product: seed author, flower team member, fruit author.
- **B21** An evaluator of the product's hackathon gets the details of an unpublished flower and fruit, within the
  public set plus `driveLink` and `template`; only `id`, `state` and `title` are guaranteed.
- **B22** An evaluator asking for an unpublished seed → `404`; an evaluator of another hackathon asking for an
  unpublished flower or fruit → `404`.
- **B23** An evaluator of the product's hackathon gets a published flower's and fruit's `driveLink` and `template`.
- **B6** Unknown id → `404`, for each type.
- **B7** A guest gets the details of a published flower and fruit of a private hackathon.

Seed fields
- **B8** Guest and participant get exactly `id`, `title`, `state`, `branchesOfKnowledge`, `seedPDF` (a link),
  `mainImage` (a link), `authors`, plus optional `videoLink`, `presentationLink`, `podcastLink`, `otherImages`; no
  `driveLink`, no `template`.
- **B9** A seed stored with video, presentation and podcast links returns those links.
- **B10** Staff and the seed's authors get the same set plus `driveLink` and `template`, and nothing else.

Flower fields
- **B11** Guest and participant get exactly `id`, `title`, `concept`, `state`, `seed`, `mainImage` (a link),
  `authors`, plus optional `prototypeImages`, `inspirationImages`, `presentation`, `video`; no `driveLink`, no
  `template`.
- **B12** `seed` is the flower's original seed as its own details (`GET /seeds/{id}`) give it to the same viewer:
  - it equals that response, for each actor;
  - guest and participant: exactly the B8 set, no `driveLink`, no `template`;
  - staff and the seed's authors: the B8 set plus `driveLink` and `template` (B10);
  - a team member of the flower who is not one of the seed's authors: no `driveLink`, no `template`.
- **B13** Staff and the flower's team members get the same set plus `driveLink` and `template`, and nothing else.

Fruit fields
- **B14** Guest and participant get exactly `id`, `title`, `state`, `authorVision`, `curatorVision`, `flower`,
  `seed`, `mainImage` (a link), `authors`, plus optional `otherImages`; no `driveLink`, no `template`.
- **B15** `flower` is the fruit's flower as its own details (`GET /flowers/{id}`) give it to the same viewer, without
  its `seed`:
  - it equals that response minus `seed`, for each actor;
  - guest and participant: exactly the B11 set minus `seed`, no `driveLink`, no `template`;
  - staff and the flower's team members: the same plus `driveLink` and `template` (B13).
- **B16** `seed` is the flower's seed as its own details (`GET /seeds/{id}`) give it to the same viewer, plus
  `description`:
  - it equals that response plus `description`, for each actor;
  - `description` is the fruit's own seed description, not the seed's data;
  - guest and participant: exactly the B8 set plus `description`, no `driveLink`, no `template`;
  - staff: the same plus `driveLink` and `template`;
  - the fruit's author, who is not one of the seed's authors: no `driveLink`, no `template`.
- **B17** Staff and the fruit's authors get the same set plus `driveLink` and `template`, and nothing else.

Every type
- **B18** Each author is exactly `id`, `name`, `surname`, and the authors are the product's own.
- **B19** Anyone who is neither staff nor an author of a published product gets no `driveLink` and no `template`,
  for each type: a participant, an inspiring scientist of the product's edition, an evaluator (for a flower or fruit:
  of another hackathon).
- **B20** Staff and authors get an unpublished product's details within the same allowed set; only `id`, `state`
  and `title` are guaranteed, the rest may be absent.

## Fixture notes
- Seeds: `aSeed({ edition, authors, state, videoLink, presentationLink, podcastLink })`, `aPublishedSeed`. A seed
  has a main image and a PDF link by default.
- Flowers: a hackathon in a team phase or ended, with the seed assigned (`seeds: [seed]`) and the members enrolled;
  then `aTeam({ hackathon, seed, members, flowerState })`. Delivered and published flowers have a title and concept.
- Fruits: `aFruit({ edition, hackathon, authors, state, authorVision, curatorVision, seedDescription })` builds the
  whole chain and returns its `flower`, `seed` and `hackathon` (B15, B16). The fruit's authors are its flower's team
  members; its seed has no authors.
- A private hackathon: `isPrivate: true` (B7). Authors are participants (`participant(n)`).
- Inspiring scientist of the edition: `aScientistOf(edition, user)`. Evaluator: `anEvaluatorOf(hackathon, user)`, or
  `evaluators: [user]` on the hackathon (B19, B21–B23). A flower's hackathon is its team's; a fruit's is the
  `hackathon` `aFruit` returns.
