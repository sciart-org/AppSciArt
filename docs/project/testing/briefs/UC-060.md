## Requirement
- UC-060 Manage group presentations (`srs-export.txt` ~line 1871): "…when a facilitator wants to control which
  exploring group's conceptual map is currently being presented…" Steps: the system shows the groups and the one
  presenting; the facilitator selects the next group; the system sets it as presenting. Pending SRS change (D-049
  Q12): "Conceptual maps have been delivered" becomes a UI warning, not a precondition.
- UC-051 Present map (~line 1713): the presenting group's map is shown to the hackathon.
- IRQ-006 Hackathon information (~line 2408): the current presenting group is part of the hackathon's state.
- BRU-025 Evaluators' activity (~line 2779): evaluators observe, and act only on rubrics.

## Clarifications
- `docs/project/clarifications/UC-047.md`: any enrolled participant can enter, assisting or not.
- `docs/project/clarifications/UC-066.md`: evaluators see the management view read-only.
- `docs/project/clarifications/general.md` "Actors": staff = facilitators, designers and administrators.
- User, 2026-11-24: each hackathon has one room for its participants; presentations reach every participant who
  joined it.

## Vocabulary
- Ongoing hackathon in group presentations = `anOngoingHackathonAt('GROUP_PRESENTATION', …)`.
- Joining = `socketAs(user, { hackathon })`. Participants join the hackathon's room; staff and evaluators also join
  its management room.
- Every event below is answered by the server once handled, refused or not: send it with
  `await socket.timeout(2000).emitWithAck(event, ...args)`. A refusal sends `error_message` (a text) to the sender
  and changes nothing. Broadcasts are not sent back to the sender.
- Groups are numbered 1, 2, … within the hackathon.
- `set_presenting_group` with a group number: everyone else who joined receives `new_presenting_group` with that
  number.
- `get_group_presenting_state`: the sender receives `group_presenting_state`; its `presentingGroup` is the current
  number. Check only that field.
- `enable_ratings_submission` (no arguments): everyone else who joined receives `ratings_submission_enabled`.

## Test cases
Setting the presenting group
- **B1** A facilitator sets group 2: participants who joined receive `new_presenting_group` with `2`, whether or not
  they are assisting or in a group.
- **B2** Another staff member and an evaluator who joined receive `new_presenting_group` with `2`.
- **B3** A designer and an administrator can each set the presenting group.
- **B4** A client that joins after the change gets `presentingGroup` `2` from `get_group_presenting_state` (IRQ-006).
- **B5** A participant's `set_presenting_group` gets `error_message`; nobody receives `new_presenting_group`, and the
  presenting group is unchanged when read back.
- **B6** An evaluator's `set_presenting_group` gets `error_message`, with the same checks (BRU-025).
- **B7** A participant who joined another hackathon doesn't receive this hackathon's `new_presenting_group`.

Opening ratings
- **B8** A facilitator's `enable_ratings_submission` reaches the participants who joined as
  `ratings_submission_enabled`.
- **B9** A participant's and an evaluator's `enable_ratings_submission` each get `error_message`, and nobody
  receives `ratings_submission_enabled`.

Bad requests
- **B10** `set_presenting_group` with a malformed group number (`0`, `-1`, `'2'`, missing) gets `error_message`, the
  presenting group is unchanged, and the server keeps running (`serverCrashes()` is empty).
- **B11** `set_presenting_group`, `enable_ratings_submission` and `get_group_presenting_state` from a socket that
  joined no hackathon each get `error_message`, and the server keeps running.
- **B12** `set_presenting_group` with a well-formed number that matches no group of the hackathon (`99`) gets
  `error_message`, nobody receives `new_presenting_group`, and the presenting group is unchanged when read back.

## Fixture notes
- `anOngoingHackathonAt('GROUP_PRESENTATION', { edition, seeds, participants, assisting, evaluators })`, two groups
  (`aGroup`) on two seeds of that edition (`aPublishedSeed`), plus an enrolled participant in no group.
- B7: a second ongoing hackathon with its own participant.
- Use a new hackathon per test: the presenting group is kept per hackathon.
